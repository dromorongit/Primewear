About Prime Wear

Home / About Us

Your Trusted Fashion Partner Since inception

Welcome to Prime Wear Wholesale, your premier destination for high-quality fashion products in Accra, Ghana. Located in the heart of Dome, we've established ourselves as a trusted name in the wholesale and retail fashion industry.

Our mission is simple: to provide retailers and individual customers with access to premium fashion products at competitive prices. We believe that quality fashion should be accessible to everyone, which is why we source our products carefully and maintain strict quality standards.

Whether you're a boutique owner looking to stock your shelves or an individual seeking trendy fashion pieces, Prime Wear has something for everyone. Our extensive collection includes clothing and accessories for all tastes and budgets.

Our Story

Founded with a vision to revolutionize the fashion wholesale industry in Ghana, Prime Wear has grown from a small boutique to a leading fashion supplier.

We understand the challenges faced by retailers in finding quality products at reasonable prices. That's why we've built relationships with trusted suppliers worldwide to bring you the best fashion products.

Our commitment to quality, customer service, and competitive pricing has earned us the trust of hundreds of customers across Ghana and beyond.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Quality First

We never compromise on quality. Every product in our inventory meets our high standards for craftsmanship and durability.

Trust & Integrity

We build lasting relationships with our customers through honest dealings, transparent pricing, and reliable service.

Customer Focus

Our customers' needs are at the heart of everything we do. We listen, adapt, and continuously improve to serve you better.
